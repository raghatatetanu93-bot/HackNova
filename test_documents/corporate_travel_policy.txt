CORPORATE EMPLOYEE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Section 3: Reimbursement Limits
Employees traveling on authorized company business are eligible for a daily per diem allowance of $85.00 for meals and incidentals.
Lodging expenses in Tier-1 metropolitan areas are capped at $280.00 per night excluding local taxes.
Itemized receipts must be submitted via the expense portal within 14 calendar days of trip conclusion.
